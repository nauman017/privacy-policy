Privacy Policy

Esports Trainer Pro Academy
Last Updated  ·  April 23, 2026
Overview
FZ Developers, the developer of Esports Trainer Pro Academy, respects your privacy. This app is designed to work completely offline and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No account, login, or registration is required to use the app.
Information We Collect
We do not collect any personal or sensitive information from users, including name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, device information, or payment details. The app functions without requiring any personal data input from the user.
Local Data Storage
The app may store certain data locally on your device, such as training progress, preferences, and settings. This data remains stored only on your device and is never uploaded, transmitted, or accessed by us or any external party.
How We Use Data
The locally stored data is used only to improve your experience, save your progress, and provide personalized training content within the app.
Drill Timer & Background Service
This app includes a Drill Timer feature that plays audio alerts and runs a foreground service to keep the timer active while your screen is off or the app is in the background. This requires the FOREGROUND_SERVICE and POST_NOTIFICATIONS permissions. No data is collected or transmitted during this process. The timer runs entirely on your device.
Notifications
The app may send local notifications to alert you when a drill timer completes. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any external servers or data collection.
Third-Party Services
This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking tools. No external services are integrated that collect or process user data.
Data Security
Since all data remains stored locally on your device, security depends on your device's built-in protection. We do not have access to your data at any point.
Your Rights
You can clear all stored data at any time through your device settings. Uninstalling the app will remove all stored data.
Data Sharing
We do not sell, share, or transfer any user data to third parties under any circumstances. All information stays strictly on the user's device and remains private.
Data Deletion
You can delete all stored data at any time by clearing the app data from your device settings or by uninstalling the app. This will permanently remove all locally stored information.
Children's Privacy
This app does not knowingly collect any personal data from any users, including children.
Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Continued use of the app means you accept any updates.
